    Create an account

    No account

    You install, you use. There is no FLOW account: no sign-up, no password.

    Show ads

    No ads

    Nothing interrupts a session: no banner, no video to watch before you can continue.

    Collect your data

    No tracking

    No data is collected. What you write stays on your devices; between your iPhone and your Mac, it travels through your own iCloud.

    Require a connection

    Offline first

    On the subway, on a plane or in the countryside: the content is in the app. FLOW has no server to depend on.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is FLOW?

    A family of apps for iPhone and Mac. Each app does one single thing: count your glasses of water, prepare tomorrow, learn Italian. They share the same look and the same principles.

    Where can I download the apps?

    Anglais is the first to reach the App Store; Italien, Espagnole and Allemand follow. The other apps are in preparation.

    Do I need to create an account?

    No. There is no FLOW account. A few apps connect to a service you already use, such as your calendar or Apple Health: you are the one who allows it, on your device.

    Where does my data go?

    Nowhere. It stays on your devices. Between your iPhone and your Mac, it goes through your own iCloud. FLOW has no server.

    Does it work without a network?

    For the most part, yes: the content is in the app. Only the apps that read an online service need the network to do so.

    iPhone or Mac?

    Almost all of them exist on both, with the same screens, and sync. A few live only on the Mac or only on the iPhone: it is shown under each app.

    Is it paid?

    Anglais is completely free. The price of the other apps will be given when they are released.

    What language are the apps in?

    French, for now. This page is translated; the apps themselves are in French.
